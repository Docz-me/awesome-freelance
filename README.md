## Getting Paid

### Invoicing & Billing

- [Docz.me](https://docz.me) - All-in-one for freelancers: contracts, invoices, time tracking, and auto-chase.
- [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com) - Contracts, invoices, and proposals in one app.
- [FreshBooks](https://www.freshbooks.com) - Cloud accounting with strong invoicing.
- [Flowlancerr Invoice Generator](https://www.flowlancerr.com/free-invoice-generator) - Free invoice generator for freelancers: no signup, no watermark, print-ready PDF.
- [Invoice Ninja](https://www.invoiceninja.com) - Open-source invoicing platform.
- [Wave](https://www.waveapps.com) - Free invoicing and accounting.

### Payment Processing

- [Mollie](https://www.mollie.com) - European payment methods (iDEAL, SEPA, Bancontact).
- [Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com) - Cross-border payouts and marketplace integrations.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com) - Card payments, recurring billing, global coverage.
- [Wise](https://wise.com) - Multi-currency receiving accounts in 10+ currencies.

### Late Payment Recovery

- [Chaser](https://www.chaserhq.com) - Automated invoice chasing with templated escalation.
- [Satago](https://www.satago.com) - Credit risk scoring plus chasing.

## Contracts & Legal

- [Bonsai Contracts](https://www.hellobonsai.com/contracts) - Lawyer-vetted freelance templates.
- [DocuSign](https://www.docusign.com) - Industry-standard e-signature.
- [Docz.me Contracts](https://docz.me) - Templates, e-signatures, storage in one workflow.
- [HelloSign](https://www.hellosign.com) - E-signatures by Dropbox.
- [StationeryHQ](https://stationeryhq.com) - Free contract templates by industry.

## Time Tracking

- [Clockify](https://clockify.me) - Free unlimited time tracking.
- [Harvest](https://www.getharvest.com) - Time tracking with built-in invoicing.
- [RescueTime](https://www.rescuetime.com) - Automatic time tracking and focus reports.
- [Toggl Track](https://toggl.com/track) - Clean, fast timer plus reports.

## Project & Client Management

- [ClickUp](https://clickup.com) - Project management with invoicing add-ons.
- [Linear](https://linear.app) - Issue tracking that doesn't suck.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so) - All-purpose workspace; client portals via shared pages.
- [Trello](https://trello.com) - Visual kanban for solo workflows.

## Accounting & Taxes

- [FreeAgent](https://www.freeagent.com) - UK-focused, MTD-ready.
- [Keeper Tax](https://www.keepertax.com) - Auto-categorize expenses for taxes.
- [QuickBooks Self-Employed](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/self-employed) - US-focused, integrates with TurboTax.
- [Xero](https://www.xero.com) - Strong international support and bank feeds.

## Finding Work

### Job Boards

- [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com) - High-volume remote jobs.
- [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com) - Remote-first job board.
- [Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com) - Curated remote jobs.

### Marketplaces

- [Codementor](https://www.codementor.io) - Developer-focused, mentorship and freelance gigs.
- [Contra](https://contra.co) - Commission-free freelance platform.
- [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com) - Top-3% screened freelancers, premium rates.
- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com) - Largest general marketplace.

### Direct Outreach

- [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io) - Lead database plus outreach.
- [Hunter](https://hunter.io) - Find verified email addresses.
- [Lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com) - Cold email automation with personalization.

## Productivity

- [Arc](https://arc.net) - Browser built around tabs-as-workspace.
- [Bartender](https://www.macbartender.com) - Tame your macOS menu bar.
- [Cron](https://cron.com) - Calendar built for power users.
- [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com) - Spotlight replacement with extensions.

## Learning

### Books

- [Company of One](https://ofone.co) by Paul Jarvis - Why staying small can be the goal.
- [The Freelancer's Bible](https://www.workingsolo.com) by Sara Horowitz - Comprehensive intro.
- [The Pricing Roadmap](https://thepricingroadmap.com) by Ulrik Lehrskov-Schmidt - B2B pricing strategy.
- [The War of Art](https://stevenpressfield.com/the-war-of-art/) by Steven Pressfield - On creative resistance.

### Newsletters

- [Freelance Brain](https://www.freelancebrain.com) - Weekly tips for freelancers.
- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com) - Weekly digest for indie founders.
- [The Hustle](https://thehustle.co) - Daily business news for self-employed.

### Podcasts

- [Being Freelance](https://beingfreelance.com) - Long-running freelance interview show.
- [Indie Hackers Podcast](https://www.indiehackers.com/podcast) - Founder interviews.
- [The Freelance Podcast](https://www.thefreelancepodcast.com) - Weekly freelancer interviews.

### Courses

- [Freelance Bold](https://freelancebold.com) - End-to-end freelance business curriculum.
